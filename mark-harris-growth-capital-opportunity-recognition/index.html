Mark Harris – Growth Capital & Opportunity Recognition (Day 2)

(October 23, 2012) During this second from a series of three lectures, Mark Harris discusses how young entrepreneurs can find growth capital and how they can recognize business opportunities. Professor Harris is a seasoned executive in the IT industry. Having recently retired from Intel, Professor Harris founded InnovaVentures to follow his passion for entrepreneurship and new venture creation.
