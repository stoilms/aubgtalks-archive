Most of them don’t even try to include the idea of God in the modern science. And of course, there are exceptions such as Dr. Michael Strauss, who tries to find a scientific explanation of God in his talk “God, Particles and the Cosmos”.  Dr. Michael Strauss is the Professor of Physics at the University of Oklahoma.
