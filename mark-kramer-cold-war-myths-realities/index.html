Mark Kramer – Cold War Myths & Realities

(February 26, 2014) Harvard professor Mark Kramer talks how today’s threats and challenges actually stem from the Cold War. Watch the video to learn about common misconceptions and misinformation about the Cold War. Professor Kramer also answers questions from AUBG students and faculty. Professor Kramer is Director of the Cold War Studies program at Harvard University.
