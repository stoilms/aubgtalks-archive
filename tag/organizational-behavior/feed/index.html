Veneta Andonova, Professor of Business at AUBG, talked about the PESTEL analysis method. Asli Goksoy, also a Business faculty at AUBG, talked about the different classical models of organizational culture. Jana Schmutzler, PhD student at Bergische Universität Wuppertal, presented the newest findings in the field, after which all three professors answered questions from the audience.
