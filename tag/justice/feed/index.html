Farrell presents his thoughts on the ICC and proposes new approaches to international law. Professor Farrell is Director of Academic Achievement and Adjunct Lecturer in Law and International Studies at The College of Law at the University of Iowa and has also conducted human rights field research in the Middle East. Professor Farrell is currently teaching at Sofia University as a Fulbright Scholar and is giving lectures around Bulgaria.
