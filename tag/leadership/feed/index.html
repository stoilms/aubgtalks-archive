Since graduating from the American University in Bulgaria in 2009, Velina has been a consultant, career coach and entrepreneur. She is the founder of the Center for Genuine People & Change, and is currently working as a consultant at PwC. After recently completing her MBA at London School of Economics, Velina returns to AUBG to present leadership theories and her advice for the students.
