(April 12, 2013) AUBG alumnus Mikhail Nahorny talks about what corporate finance is like in the real world. Nahorny is currently a Vice President of Borealis, the infrastructure investment arm of OMERS, one of Canada’s largest pension funds with net assets of more than CAD 60 billion and a AAA credit rating. Nahorny graduated from AUBG in 2000 and he has since worked for Royal Dutch Shell, Citigroup and the Royal Bank of Canada, before joining Borealis Infrastructure in 2011.
