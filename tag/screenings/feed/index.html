Apostolof, a Bulgarian-American filmmaker specializing in low-budget exploitation and erotic films. Jordan Todorov, Director of the movie, visited the American University in Bulgaria in October 2012 to screen the film. In this Q&A session, Todorov talks about the movie and answers interesting questions from Journalism students.
